## Values

| Value |  |
| --- | --- |
| Quality and integrity | “Quality and integrity” unesco “Reproducibility and scrutiny are essential to safeguard the quality and integrity of scientific work” npos |
| Collective benefit | “Collective benefit” unesco “Scientific knowledge is a public good and access to it is a universal right ” npos |
| Equity, fairness, diversity and inclusion | “Equity and fairness” unesco “Diversity and inclusiveness” unesco “Diversity, equity, and inclusiveness are crucial for the success of Open Science” npos |

Values can be seen as shared commitments. The values of the UNESCO and NPOS are taken as a point of departure for developing the principles for the Open Science infrastructure. Principles and subprinciples as actionable guidelines that can be made operational (actions, enablers, practices or consequences).
